Privacy Policy for FintaroFlow

Last updated: February 21, 2026

FintaroFlow ("the App") is a personal expense tracker. This Privacy Policy explains what data the App processes, where it is stored, and what control you have.

1. Data We Process

The App may process the data you create inside it, including:

  • Transactions (amounts, dates, notes, categories, currencies)
  • Recurring transaction rules
  • Categories (names, icons, colors)
  • App settings (theme, period options, widget preferences, lock settings)

2. Storage Model

  • By default, data is stored locally on your device (offline-first).
  • No advertising SDKs are used.
  • No analytics or tracking SDKs are used.

3. Optional Dropbox Sync

If you enable Dropbox sync, the App uses Dropbox OAuth authorization and uploads exported CSV app data to your Dropbox account. This is optional and controlled by you. Dropbox processing is governed by Dropbox terms and privacy policy.

4. CSV Import and Export

The App can import/export CSV files when you explicitly select files through the Android file picker. Import/export may include transaction notes and category names that you entered.

5. Biometric Lock

If enabled, biometric authentication is used only to protect app access on your device. Biometric templates are managed by Android and are not collected, stored, or transmitted by the App.

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell personal data. Data is shared only when you explicitly enable an external sync destination or when legally required.

7. Retention and Deletion

  • You can edit and delete data within the App.
  • You can clear data in-app or by uninstalling the App.
  • Deleted items may remain temporarily in recycle-bin style features if enabled.

8. Security

Reasonable measures are used to protect app-managed data. No software storage or transfer method can guarantee absolute security.

9. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

10. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated from time to time. Material changes are reflected by updating the date above.

11. Contact

For privacy questions, contact: fintaroflow@protonmail.com
